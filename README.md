# Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Portfolio

Welcome to my master data portfolio. This repository serves as a comprehensive showcase of my technical expertise in data engineering, advanced business intelligence, and executive reporting. 

The projects housed here are divided into two categories: **Professional Equivalents** (simulating enterprise-level workflows from my corporate internships) and **Exploratory Personal Projects** (driven by complex datasets and emerging analytical frameworks).

---

## Professional Equivalents (Internship Simulation)

Due to strict corporate confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) from my previous internships, I cannot host proprietary data or production code publicly. To demonstrate my capabilities, I have meticulously reverse-engineered the exact workflow complexities, business logic, and architectural designs from my corporate experience using open-source **Kaggle datasets**. 

These enterprise-grade simulations focus exclusively on **Excel, Power BI, VBA, and PowerPoint** workflows, mirroring the functional and visual quality expected in a corporate environment:

*   **Automated Data Pipelines (Excel & VBA):** Custom-built macros designed to eliminate manual data entry, optimize workbook performance, and clean raw data inputs into structured, high-integrity models.
*   **Enterprise Dashboarding (Power BI):** Interactive, user-centric report interfaces that transform multi-layered transactional data into clean, scannable KPIs and executive summaries.
*   **Executive Presentation Decks (PowerPoint):** High-impact data storytelling assets designed to translate technical data insights into strategic, boardroom-ready recommendations.

> *Note: Technical programming workflows (Python, SQL architecture, etc.) are excluded from this specific repository due to the proprietary complexity of their industrial environments.*
